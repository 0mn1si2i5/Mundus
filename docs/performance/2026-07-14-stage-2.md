# Stage 2 release regression · 2026-07-14

## Scope and method

- Production build served with `pnpm preview`
- Headed Google Chrome 147.0.7727.56 on the same Apple M5 Pro MacBook Pro as the desktop baseline
- 1200 × 1471 CSS viewport, DPR 2
- Automatically selected quality profile: `medium`
- 2 seconds warm-up followed by 6 seconds of actual R3F render-frame sampling
- Final Other Side slice active, including the lazily loaded Natural Earth nearest-place result

The benchmark records frames inside R3F's `useFrame`. The structured result was read from `window.__MUNDUS_BENCHMARK__` after the visible panel reached `complete`.
